### Prompt 01: Rapid Residency Phase IV — Foothill Dawn & Architecture
* **Scene**: Early morning dawn over the Matheran foothills revealing the contemporary residential elevation of Rapid Residency Phase IV in Neral.
* **Camera Movement**: Slow forward ascending drone dolly (`dolly_in + crane_up`), starting close to lush green tree canopies at the foothills of Matheran, rising steadily to frame the modern multi-storey façade washed in soft golden sunrise light.
* **Lens**: 35mm anamorphic prime, subtle horizontal flare, shallow depth-of-field resolving into razor-sharp architectural clarity.
* **Lighting**: Natural blue hour transitioning into golden hour (warm 3200K rim lighting against cool 6500K mountain atmosphere), delicate morning mist dissipating through balconies.
* **Environment**: Dew-covered subtropical greenery, distant undulating ridges of the Matheran range, crisp clean sky, warm interior lamps visible through floor-to-ceiling balcony glazing.
* **Technical Parameters**:
  * **Duration**: 8 seconds
  * **Frame Rate**: 24 fps
  * **Aspect Ratio**: 2.39:1 widescreen (and 16:9 4K)
  * **Motion Score**: Smooth, stately, zero jitter (Motion index: 3)
  * **Looping**: Seamless start-to-end luminosity match
* **Full Prompt String**:
  ```text
  Hyper-cinematic 8k architectural drone reveal of Rapid Residency Phase IV residential building at the foothills of Matheran, Neral. Camera executes a stately, continuous forward crane ascension rising through morning mist over verdant lush trees to reveal warm cream and rich brown textured architectural façades. Floor-to-ceiling glass balconies catch early morning golden sunrise glints. Background reveals majestic layered mountain ridges under soft pastel dawn sky. 35mm anamorphic lens, subtle anamorphic streak flare, Photorealistic, ultra-high dynamic range, steady gimbal motion, 24fps, no humans, pristine architectural design, Aman Resorts aesthetic.
  ```
